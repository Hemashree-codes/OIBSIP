A LIFE OF PURPOSE
01 / HIS STORY

A life dedicated
to the nation.

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Growing up in a modest family, he developed a deep interest in learning, mathematics and science.

He studied aerospace engineering and became a respected scientist. His work at India's defence and space research organisations helped advance the country's missile and space programmes. He became widely known as the "Missile Man of India."

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India, serving until 2007. He connected with students across the country and encouraged young people to learn, think creatively and work towards a developed India.

On 27 July 2015, he passed away while delivering a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong. His life continues to inspire people through his scientific work, books and commitment to education.
